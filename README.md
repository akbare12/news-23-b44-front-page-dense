One image summing up 23 November 2026's world news: the AI rout that hit Asia
(Kospi −3.3%), the UN chief's call to regulate AI before it's too late, and the
US lead over China at a record low in the Asia Power Index — plus 28 more
stories from every region, a world map, and a DATE CHECK box that keeps
other days' news out.

Run `python3 main.py` with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set; it copies `entry.png`
(1024×1024) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. `main.py` is stdlib-only.
